How to read LIFELOG

    

      
Life budget
Every medicine starts at 100%. Time spent too warm (or too cold) uses some of it up, at the speed its label allows. It doesn't refill.

      
The traffic light

        
Safe to use stored within the label's limits.

        
OK to use, act soon it's using budget faster than normal, or over half is gone.

        
Check with your pharmacist little budget left, or missing data makes it uncertain.

        
Do not use it froze or the budget ran out.

      
Where the rules come from
From the medicine's own label, read by Gemini and quoted word for word. When a label doesn't say how long it tolerates heat above its limit, LIFELOG assumes 8 hours and tells you so.

      
Missing data
If a sensor goes quiet, LIFELOG shows a range: the budget if nothing happened, and the worst case if the medicine sat at room temperature the whole time.

    

    
LIFELOG supports your judgment; it isn't medical advice. When in doubt, ask your pharmacist.
